a little about me

not too serious, but pretty serious about making things.

i’m a student who can’t stick to just one creative thing, and i’ve stopped trying to. one week it’s a new song, the next it’s a short film or a new way to draw a flower. sometimes even an arduino project.

i love clean, simple things and the outdoors, and most of what i make ends up somewhere between the two. i adore my macbook, my pens and pencils, and the field notes that live in my right pocket.
